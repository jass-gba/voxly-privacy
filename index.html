Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VOXLY ("VOXLY", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the VOXLY mobile application.

VOXLY provides voice recording, audio cleaning, noise removal, voice enhancement, playback, and audio file saving features.

Your privacy is important to us. We aim to collect and process only the information necessary to provide the features of VOXLY.

1. Information We Collect

VOXLY does not require you to create an account to use its basic recording and audio processing features.

Depending on the features you use, VOXLY may access:

  • Microphone audio when you start a voice recording.
  • Audio files created or selected for processing through the application.
  • Local application data required for the application to operate correctly.

2. Microphone Permission

VOXLY requires microphone permission to provide its voice recording functionality.

The microphone is accessed when you use a recording feature. The recorded audio is used to create the recording requested by you.

You can revoke microphone permission at any time through your Android device settings. Revoking the permission may prevent recording features from working.

3. Voice Recordings

Voice recordings created through VOXLY are user-generated audio content.

Recordings may be stored on your device so that you can play, process, export, or otherwise use them through the application's available features.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have appropriate permission or consent before recording another person.

4. Audio Processing

VOXLY may process audio recordings to provide features such as:

  • Noise removal
  • Voice cleaning
  • Voice enhancement
  • Volume adjustment
  • Audio normalization
  • Audio playback
  • Audio file generation and saving

These processing operations are performed to provide the functionality requested by the user.

5. Local Audio Processing

VOXLY's core voice recording and audio processing features are designed to work locally on the user's device where applicable.

When processing is performed locally, the audio does not need to be uploaded to a remote server for that processing.

If future versions of VOXLY introduce cloud-based processing or online AI services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how audio and other information are handled.

6. Storage

VOXLY may store recordings and processed audio files on your device.
