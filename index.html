Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AUDNORA handles information when you use the AUDNORA Android application.

AUDNORA is designed to help users record, clean, enhance and listen to their own voice recordings. We respect your privacy and aim to keep your audio and personal information under your control.

In simple words: AUDNORA does not sell your personal information. Your voice recordings are intended to remain on your device unless you choose to share or transfer them yourself.

1. Information We Collect

AUDNORA is designed to operate with minimal data collection.

Depending on how you use the application, AUDNORA may access:

  • Voice/audio recordings that you create or select within the app.
  • Audio files that you choose to process or play.
  • Basic technical information required for the application to function properly.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, financial information, contacts or private messages through AUDNORA.

2. Voice Recordings and Audio Files

AUDNORA requires access to your device microphone when you choose to record audio.

Voice recordings are processed for the purpose of providing the application's recording, cleaning and enhancement features.

Your recordings are not intentionally uploaded to our servers simply because you use the application. Processed audio files may be stored on your device so that you can listen to or use them later.

You remain responsible for the audio files you create, import, save or share.

3. Microphone Permission

AUDNORA may request microphone permission because recording voice is one of its core functions.

The microphone is accessed when you use the recording functionality. You can manage microphone permission through your Android device settings.

4. Storage and Files

AUDNORA may create, read or save audio files when necessary to provide recording, audio cleaning, enhancement and playback functionality.

Files created by the application are intended to remain on your device unless you choose to share, copy, upload or otherwise transfer them.

5. Data Sharing

AUDNORA does not sell or rent your personal information.

We do not intentionally share your voice recordings with advertisers or other companies for their own purposes.

If you use Android's sharing features or another application to share an audio file, that file will be handled according to the privacy policy of the service or application you choose.

6. Internet Connection

Certain versions or features of AUDNORA may require an internet connection.

If the application does not require an online service for a particular audio-processing function, your audio may be processed locally on your device.

Network activity may also occur when required by the Android operating system, app distribution services or other integrated services.

7. Third-Party Services

AUDNORA may rely on third-party platforms or services for distribution, hosting, analytics, crash reporting or other technical functionality in future versions.

Such third-party services may process limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

AUDNORA does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services. Users should review the applicable policies of those services when appropriate.

8. Advertising

AUDNORA does not intentionally collect personal information for the purpose of selling it to advertisers.

If advertising is introduced into a future version of the application, the relevant advertising provider's privacy practices may also apply. This Privacy Policy may be updated when such features are introduced.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by AUDNORA. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should keep their Android device secure and avoid sharing private audio files with untrusted applications or people.

10. Children's Privacy

AUDNORA is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user profiles or selling information.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation.

11. Your Control

You can control application permissions through your Android device settings. You can also delete audio recordings and files from your device when you no longer need them.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to AUDNORA, new features, legal requirements or improvements to our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
